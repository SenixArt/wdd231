Site Plan: Mundo Creativo Digital

Daniel Cosme | WDD 231

Site Name

Name: Mundo Creativo Digital

Reasoning: This name represents an e-commerce and learning hub that provides digital design assets, templates, and courses for creative designers, craft makers, and online entrepreneurs.

Site Purpose

The site serves as a dynamic web portal where users can browse digital assets, filter design resources, view course information, and submit contact inquiries for custom design branding services.

Target Audience Scenarios

  • "Where can I find customizable vector templates and digital assets for my craft business?"
  • "How can I enroll in online design courses for specialized tools like Figma and Silhouette Cameo?"

Color Scheme

The selected color scheme establishes a modern and creative brand identity while maintaining high contrast for web accessibility.

Typography

Heading Font: Montserrat (Used for primary titles, section headings, and navigation menu links).

Body Font: Open Sans (Used for general page body copy, descriptions, forms, and lists for optimal readability).
